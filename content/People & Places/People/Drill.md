Drill (also known as "Adriel" or "Adrill") is a founding member of [[5BB Productions]]. He is also an original member of 5pee But Big DND and plays Drittle in [[Campaign Two 5BB The World Blooms|Campaign Two: 5BB The World Blooms]].

He portrays Primal Darkness in _Sid Man_.

He is the author and main illustrator of Greg.

He was the Dungeon Master of Campaign One.
