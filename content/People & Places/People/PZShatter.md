PZShatter (also known as "PZ" or "Ling Ling") is an original member of [[5pee But Big DND]] and plays Draek Edoric in [[Campaign Two 5BB The World Blooms|Campaign Two: 5BB The World Blooms]].

In Campaign One, PZ played poopee man, Damian, Pizz A. Fart and Dielan.
