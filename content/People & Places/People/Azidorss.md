Azidorss (also known as "Azi") is a founding member of [[5BB Productions]]. He is also an original member of 5pee But Big DND and is currently the Dungeon Master of [[Campaign Two 5BB The World Blooms|Campaign Two: 5BB The World Blooms]].

He portrays himself in _Sid Man_.

In Campaign One, Azi played the character Jack Walker.
