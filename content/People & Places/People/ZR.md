ZR (also known as "LuckyTaco" or "LuckiestOfTacos") is a founding member of [[5BB Productions]]. He is also an original member of 5pee But Big DND and plays Hermes in [[Campaign Two 5BB The World Blooms|Campaign Two: 5BB The World Blooms]].

He portrays himself in _Sid Man_.

In Campaign One, ZR played the character Sell Dino Nuggets To Rule The World.
