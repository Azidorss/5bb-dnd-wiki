Bird (also known as "czgb") is a founding member of [[5BB Productions]]. She is also an original member of 5pee But Big DND and plays Edwin Durland in [[Campaign Two 5BB The World Blooms|Campaign Two: 5BB The World Blooms]].

She had a solo Youtube channel, Definitions, which covered the meanings of words in alphabetical order. It was ultimately deleted for unknown reasons.

She portrayed the primary antagonists assistant in Sid Man.

In Campaign One, Bird played Bob Tan.
