YJ (also known as "Pearl Scorpion") is a member of [5BB Productions] and an original member of [[5pee But Big DND]]. He plays Matt Murdock in [[Campaign Two 5BB The World Blooms|Campaign Two: 5BB The World Blooms]].

In Campaign One, YJ played Just Dennis and Sasuke Uchiha.

He is also featured in many of Azidorss' videos.
