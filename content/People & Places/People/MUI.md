MUI (also known as "MUI_GokuDBZ") is a founding member of [[5BB Productions]]. He is also an original member of [[5pee But Big DND]] and plays Grimwald Von Tutu in [[Campaign Two 5BB The World Blooms|Campaign Two: 5BB The World Blooms]].

He portrays the title character in _Sid Man_.

In Campaign One, MUI played the character Sonic The Hedgehog 3 Watch In Theatres.

He is very stinky.
