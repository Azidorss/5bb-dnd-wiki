Grimwald Von Tutu, is a Chromatic Dragonborn and a member of the main party of [[Campaign Two 5BB The World Blooms|Campaign Two]]. He is played by [[MUI]].


## Description
<font size="5">Appearance</font>
![[Pasted image 20260417155342.png#left|230]] Grimwald is a bizarre, hybrid-looking figure with a heavily stylized and surreal appearance. His body is tall and muscular, with a well-defined torso and arms, suggesting great physical strength. Large, dragon-like wings extend from his back, dark red in color and slightly tattered along the edges, giving him a draconic or otherworldly presence. His head appears unnatural and cartoonish, bright red with a smooth, rounded shape, simple dark eyes, and an exaggerated, wide grin that contrasts sharply with his otherwise realistic physique. His outfit is equally unconventional: he wears a layered, pastel-colored tutu around his waist, paired with tight black leggings that emphasize his legs. On his feet are vivid green high-heeled shoes, adding to the absurd and flamboyant nature of his design.
