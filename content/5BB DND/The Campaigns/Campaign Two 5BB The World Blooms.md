## 5BB: The World Blooms
Campaign Two is the second campaign of [[5pee But Big DND]]. It begins in Thalmeris in the year 40  A.B.C., approximately 40 years after the events of Campaign One: Drills Bullshit. The campaign features the returning cast, alongside two new members, [[Trip]] and [[Jan]]. [[Azidorss]], a player from Campaign One, takes the role of Game Master, while [[Drill]], formerly the GM, joins the table as a player for the first time. The story introduces an entirely new party of characters and takes place primarily on the continent of Marrowdeep.

## Production
The idea for Campaign Two originated around April 2025, with [[Drill]] initially planned to serve as the Game Master. 5BB originally intended to begin the campaign around June 2025. However, the group entered a hiatus during that period, delaying the conclusion of Campaign One, which in turn postponed the start of Campaign Two.

On 3 November 2025, 5BB announced that [[Azidorss]] would become the game master for Campaign Two, with [[Drill]] stepping down from the role to participate in the campaign as a player instead.

The second campaign started on March 8, 2026 with "[[Where the Paths Converge]]" <small>(2x01)</small>.


## Trivia
For Campaign Two, the table switched to a different system, using [[Dungeons & Dragons]] 5th Edition (2024). This marked a change from Campaign One, where the table had instead used a completely homebrewed system, as the players were not yet familiar with how to play the game at the time.
