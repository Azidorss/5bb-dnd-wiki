5BB Productions is a multimedia production company founded by a group of friends. The company produces the series [[5pee But Big DND]], as well as several other films, YouTube videos, and other media projects.

## History
Most of the founding members had been in a class together for two years, known as Patience (2022–2023), before they graduated. In 2024, they went their separate ways but maintained contact through Discord to continue hanging out. [[Azidorss]], who had not been in the class, joined them as a founding member.

5BB Productions was officially established in February 2024 by the members of Patience, along with [[Azidorss]]. The company was founded to produce their first film, _Sid Man_, written by [[MUI]] and directed by [[Azidorss]]. However, filming was ultimately cancelled due to scheduling conflicts, and the group decided to focus on other projects, including their DND campaigns.

## Productions

<font size="5">Series</font>
**5pee But Big DND**
 *→ Main Article: [[5pee But Big DND]]*
[[5pee But Big DND]] is the main series produced by the company. It features a group of friends playing the popular table-top game [[Dungeons & Dragons]] (often abbreviated as _D&D_) in a world imagined by the respective Dungeon Masters.

<font size="5">Films</font>
**Sid Man**
Sid Man was the very first project of the company. The idea originated in early 2022 and was first attempted in February 2024. After several weeks of discussion, the team ultimately decided to cancel filming due to scheduling conflicts.

<font size="5">Youtube Channels</font>
**Azidorss**
"Azidorss" is a YouTube channel created by [[Azidorss]] and produced in collaboration with 5BB Productions. The channel features gameplay content, including games such as _Fortnite_ and _Marvel Rivals_, with videos created alongside friends from 5BB.

**SlimeAnonymous**
SlimeAnonymous is a YouTube channel created by [[Drill]] and produced in collaboration with 5BB Productions. The channel features edits made by Drill and has collaborated with other 5BB members, including [[MUI]] and [[Azidorss]].

**MUI_GokuDBZ**
MUI\_GokuDBZ is a YouTube channel created by [[MUI]] and produced in collaboration with 5BB Productions. The channel features games such as _Roblox_ as well as edits made by MUI, and has collaborated with other 5BB members, including Drill, Azidorss, and ZR.

**LuckiestOfTacos**
LuckiestOfTacos is a YouTube channel created by [[ZR]] and produced in collaboration with 5BB Productions. The channel features _Roblox_ content and frequently includes appearances by Azidorss in its videos.

**Definitions**
Definitions was a YouTube channel created by [[Bird]] and primarily operated as a solo project. The channel focused on educational content, presenting the definitions of words in alphabetical order. It was ultimately deleted for unknown reasons.

<font size="5">Comics</font>
**Greg**
Greg is a comic series primarily written and illustrated by [[Drill]]. The series enjoyed a successful run and nearly inspired a video game adaptation.
